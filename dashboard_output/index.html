EV outlook

Considering an EV

What would move EV-curious buyers

BUSINESS INSIGHT
EV consideration is 63% among two-wheeler respondents and 59% among four-wheeler respondents. The strongest EV purchase trigger is Lower running / electricity costs for two-wheelers and Lower running costs for four-wheelers. Marketing should lead with practical ownership benefits and remove charging/affordability concerns.

Purchase decision factors

Priority index: 100 = highest priority. This replaces unreadable raw ranking strings.

BUSINESS INSIGHT
Fuel efficiency / battery range is the top two-wheeler decision factor, while Price leads four-wheelers. Product positioning and sales scripts should be segment-specific: emphasize running/ownership economics for two-wheelers and the leading value drivers for four-wheelers.

Discovery channels

Top research channels extracted from the rank columns. Higher priority index means more important.

BUSINESS INSIGHT
Social media ranks first for two-wheelers, while Friends/Relatives ranks first for four-wheelers. Allocate research-stage marketing to the channels customers actually use, with different content mixes for the two segments rather than one generic media plan.

Barriers to purchase

The strongest friction points between purchase intent and conversion.

BUSINESS INSIGHT
The leading barrier is Rising fuel costs for two-wheelers and High purchase price for four-wheelers. Use financing, total-cost-of-ownership messaging and targeted dealer support to address the highest purchase friction before asking customers to convert.

Brand attributes that matter most

What buyers want from a trustworthy brand beyond the vehicle itself.

BUSINESS INSIGHT
Two-wheeler buyers rank Fuel / energy efficiency highest, while four-wheeler buyers rank Reliability and durability highest. Position the brand around the attributes customers value most, and make those benefits visible in advertising, showroom demonstrations and sales conversations.
